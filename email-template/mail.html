Frequently asked questions
Is it beginner-friendly? Do I need prior knowledge?
Yes, absolutely. No prior experience or technical knowledge is required. Everyone is welcome.
Is it a workshop or a competition?
Both. It’s a hands-on workshop that starts with an introductory session, with a competitive element where innovation and implementation are encouraged.
Will mentors guide us?
Yes. Mentors will guide you throughout the event, and you can reach out to us for help even after it ends.
Do I need a team or a laptop?
No laptop is needed, since the hardware and components are provided. You’re welcome to carry your own notebook or laptop if you’d like. The team challenge happens towards the end, and our volunteers will help you get set up on the day.
Will there be a follow-up after the event?
Yes. We’ll send you an afternote after the event, and you can still reach us for help.
Still have questions?
Ask in the Electronova 2.0 WhatsApp group or write to us at pratyush.25bcy10188@vitbhopal.ac.in. We’re happy to help, before and after the event.
